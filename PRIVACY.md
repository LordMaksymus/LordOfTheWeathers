# WeatherEx Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026. WeatherEx is the browser extension for Lord Of The Weathers, maintained by LordMaksymus.

## Information used by the extension

The extension stores the chosen location method, city or coordinates, available place names, and display units in your browser's local extension storage. These preferences are used to restore your forecast when you reopen the popup.

If you select browser geolocation, WeatherEx asks your browser for coordinates and an accuracy estimate. The estimate is used to show the location preview. The detected coordinates are saved and used for weather requests only after you confirm them. City search and manual coordinates are available as alternatives.

## Requests to other services

- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) receives the selected latitude and longitude for weather forecasts.
- [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/), operated on OpenStreetMap infrastructure for the public endpoint used by this extension, receives a submitted city name or coordinates to look up location details. Those requests are covered by the [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- The popup's stylesheet requests fonts from [Google Fonts](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy). These requests do not contain the selected city or coordinates.

These services receive ordinary connection information, such as the IP address used for the request. WeatherEx does not use an account, its own analytics service, or advertising trackers. The maintainer does not sell location information.

## Your choices

You can change your saved location in Settings, control access to browser geolocation in your browser or operating system, or remove the extension to clear its stored preferences. Send privacy questions to the maintainer through the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/LordMaksymus/LordOfTheWeathers/issues) without including private coordinates.
